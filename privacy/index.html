Trezzzure Runners – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trezzzure handles information when you use Trezzzure Runners.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, Trezzzure Runners may process information associated with your TikTok account, gameplay and race results, personal bests, challenge and leaderboard activity, device and technical information, diagnostic information and information needed to prevent cheating or abuse.

2. Microphone and Voice

Trezzzure Runners is designed to include voice-controlled gameplay. Where this feature is enabled, the game may request permission to access your microphone so that voice input can be detected and translated into gameplay actions. We will provide appropriate permission prompts before microphone access is used.

3. Camera

Trezzzure Runners may include features that use the device camera, such as displaying the player during gameplay. Where camera functionality is enabled, the game will request the relevant permission before accessing the camera.

4. How We Use Information

We may use information to operate the game, record race results and personal bests, provide leaderboards and challenges, improve performance and reliability, prevent cheating and abuse, investigate technical problems, provide support and comply with legal or platform requirements.

5. TikTok

Trezzzure Runners may operate through TikTok and use TikTok platform services. TikTok may separately process information in accordance with its own privacy policy and platform terms. Trezzzure does not control TikTok's independent processing of information.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate Trezzzure Runners, with platform providers such as TikTok, or where required by law. Competitive information such as usernames, race times or rankings may be visible to other users where leaderboard or social features are enabled.

7. Data Retention and Security

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy and to use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access, correction or deletion and to object to or restrict certain processing. You may contact us to exercise applicable rights.

9. Children

Trezzzure Runners is subject to the age and eligibility requirements of the platforms through which it is made available. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children contrary to applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Trezzzure Runners develops, including when new features such as voice, camera, social, advertising or payment functionality are introduced. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact trezzzure25@gmail.com.
